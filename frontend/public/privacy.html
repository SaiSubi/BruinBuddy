Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

BruinBuddy is an AI study tutor for university courses, built as a UCLA class project. It is not an official UCLA service and is not affiliated with UCLA or MIT. This page explains what we collect, why, and who else handles it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and whether you signed up as a student or an instructor. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from your Google account, and nothing else (no contacts, files or calendar).
  • Course membership: the courses you join or run and your role in each.
  • What you write: your tutor chats, Ask the course questions, AI-guided assignment work, and short study notes the tutor keeps about what you are working on in each course.
  • Usage: how much AI usage each turn takes, so that course allowances and budgets work.
  • Instructor uploads: course materials that instructors upload.
  • Payments: instructors pay through Stripe. We never see or store card numbers; we keep only whether a course was paid for.

How we use it

Only to run BruinBuddy: signing you in, showing you your courses, answering your questions from your course's materials, enforcing your instructor's rules and limits, and showing course staff aggregate usage. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or advertising cookies. We do not use your messages to train AI models.

Who can see your work

  • Tutor chats are private. Course staff see only aggregate statistics (for example how many questions were asked and which materials were cited), never your individual chats, and small groups are hidden.
  • AI-guided assignments are graded work: course staff see your score, your progress and that assignment's conversation.
  • Ask the course questions are private until you, or a course rule, send them to course staff. Good answers may be added to a course FAQ without your name.

Services that process your data

  • Supabase: sign-in, database and file storage.
  • Fly.io and Vercel: hosting for the app.
  • OpenRouter and the AI model providers it routes to: your messages and the relevant course text are sent to generate answers.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Stripe: instructor payments.

Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

Storage in your browser

We keep your sign-in session and display preferences (such as theme and accessibility settings) in your browser's local storage. Nothing there is used for tracking.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your account and course data while BruinBuddy runs. You can ask us to delete your account and the data tied to it at any time using the contact below. Removing you from a course ends your access to it right away.

Children

BruinBuddy is meant for university students and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/SaiSubi/BruinBuddy/issues, or ask your course instructor to pass it on.
