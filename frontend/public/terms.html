BruinBuddy is an AI study tutor built as a UCLA class project. It is not an official UCLA service and is not affiliated with UCLA or MIT. By using it you agree to these terms.

Using BruinBuddy

  • Students join a course with the code their instructor shares. Instructors create courses, upload materials and set the rules for each problem set.
  • Keep your sign-in to yourself and give accurate account details.
  • Follow your course's academic integrity rules. Don't try to get around the tutor's limits on graded work, misuse the service, or try to access courses or data that aren't yours.

AI answers

The tutor answers from your course's materials, but it is an AI and can be wrong. Check important details against the original documents and your instructor. BruinBuddy is a study aid, not a substitute for your course.

Course materials

Instructors confirm they have the right to upload the materials they add, and keep ownership of them. Uploaded materials are used only to run that course in BruinBuddy.

Payments

Instructors pay per course through Stripe. While BruinBuddy is a class project it runs in Stripe test mode, so no real charges are made.

Access

Instructors can remove or block members of their course. We may suspend accounts that misuse the service. We may change or stop BruinBuddy at any time.
